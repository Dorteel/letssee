# 99 Archive: what I let go of

Forgetting is necessary: storage is finite, most moments don't matter, and some things (other people's private lives) I shouldn't hold on to at all. But I want to forget *on purpose* and leave a trace, so that a gap in my memory is explained rather than mysterious.

What ends up here:
- **Faded traces**: one-line summaries of Moments, Episodes or records that decayed ("2027-03-04: ~40 routine moments in the kitchen, nothing notable").
- **Retired knowledge**: beliefs and models that were disproven, with their final scorecard. Being wrong is worth remembering.
- **Deletion log**: when something was deleted for privacy, a note that a deletion happened, when and why, *without* the content.
- **Old versions**: superseded body schemas, plans and skills, for comparison.

Organised `YYYY/`. Nothing here is used for active reasoning, but it can be searched when I'm trying to understand my own past or why I no longer know something.
