# 99 Archive skills

- **Compress**: when notes from other layers decay, merge them into one trace per day and kind, keeping counts, places, and anything mildly notable.
- **Retire**: move disproven beliefs and failed models here intact, with a "why retired" line.
- **Log deletions**: for privacy deletions, write only date, kind of data, reason ("person requested", "retention expired"). Never the content.
- **Never resurrect silently**: if archived material becomes relevant again, create a new note in the right layer that links here. Don't move the archived note back.
- **Budget**: if the archive itself grows too large, compress old traces further (day → month → year).
