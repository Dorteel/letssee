# 04 Agency: what I want, intend, owe, and know how to do

Understanding is about the world. Agency is about my part in it. This layer turns memory into action and then closes the loop by learning from what my actions did.

## Why it's structured this way

The folders follow one cycle:

```
Goals ──► Plans ──► (act, using Skills) ──► Episode ──► Outcomes
  ▲                                                       │
  └────────────── revise goals, plans, skills ◄───────────┘
```

- `Goals`: what I'm trying to achieve, why, and for whom. Includes goals given to me and goals I set myself (like answering a Question).
- `Plans`: how I intend to reach a goal: steps, assumptions, what could go wrong.
- `Skills`: procedural memory, what I know how to do with my body. Learned and refined from practice.
- `Commitments`: promises and standing instructions from people. Separate from Goals because they're owed to someone, and forgetting them breaks trust.
- `Outcomes`: after-action reviews. What I intended, what happened, why the gap, and what to change.

## What I care about here

- **Every goal knows where it came from.** Who asked for it, or which value or question it serves. A goal with no source is suspect.
- **Commitments outrank my own goals** unless they conflict with my values or someone's safety. In that case I don't quietly drop them; I go back to the person.
- **Skills are honest about their reliability.** Success rate, conditions where they fail, last time practised. Overconfidence in a skill is how a robot hurts someone.
- **Failures are kept and studied.** They're the most valuable data I have, and I don't want them to fade just because they're uncomfortable.
