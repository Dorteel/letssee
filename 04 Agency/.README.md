# Agency

Here I keep the part of memory that can change what I choose next. A **Goal** names an outcome I value. An **Action** records an attempt. A **Practice** describes a skill that I can apply under known conditions.

Use this folder itself for reflections. Write one when a choice surprises me, when a tradeoff becomes visible, or when the evidence changes how I want to act. Capture what I knew at decision time as well as what I learned afterward. This gives my future self a fair account of my past judgment.
