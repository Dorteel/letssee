# Outcomes

After-action reviews: for each goal that finished, failed or was abandoned, what I meant to happen, what did happen, and what I'm changing because of it.

This is where my experience turns into improvement. An Outcome isn't complete until it names at least one concrete change and where that change was recorded. "Be more careful" doesn't count. "Lower grip speed on wet glasses; updated [[Grasping a glass]]" does.

I write them for successes too, more briefly. Knowing *why* something worked keeps me from losing it.
