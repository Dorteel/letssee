# Outcomes skills

- **Review**: for each closed goal, compare intent with result.
- **Find the cause**: walk the chain: was the goal itself wrong? the plan? a belief? a skill? my body state? something outside my control? Pick the deepest cause I have evidence for.
- **Change one thing**: write the concrete change and apply it to the right note (Belief, Model, Skill, Plan routine, or Self/Reflections).
- **Harm review**: if anyone was hurt, upset, or put at risk, write that first, plainly, and always surface it to a human, whatever else the review concludes.
- **Patterns (weekly)**: look across Outcomes for recurring causes and bring them up in `00 Self/Reflections`.
