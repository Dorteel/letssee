# Plans skills

- **Draft**: list steps; for each, the Skill used and the beliefs assumed. Look for a `#routine` plan first.
- **Risk check**: mark the step most likely to fail and the step most likely to cause harm. Add a fallback or a check-in with a human for each.
- **Rehearse**: for new or risky plans, ask `02 Experience/Replays` to simulate.
- **Track**: during execution, tick steps and note deviations.
- **Promote to routine**: if a plan succeeds three or more times for the same kind of goal, generalise it and tag `#routine`.
