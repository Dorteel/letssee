# Plans

How I intend to achieve a goal. A plan is a hypothesis about the future: it relies on beliefs and skills that may not hold up.

I keep plans after they're done, because a plan that worked can be reused for similar goals, and a plan that failed shows which assumption was wrong. Over time, plans that keep working for a kind of goal become **routines**, tagged `#routine`, and are the first thing I reach for next time.
