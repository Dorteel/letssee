# Reflect on a choice

1. Start with an action and the goal it served. Recover the expectation I recorded before acting, including any uncertainty.
2. Find a later observation that bears on the outcome. Name the interval covered. If contact or telemetry was lost, mark the outcome unknown.
3. Compare what I valued, what I expected, and what I observed. Consider another explanation for a good or bad result. An action followed by a change is one piece of evidence about cause.
4. Write one reflection. Link the expectation, action, and outcome as sources. Identify a decision I would repeat or change, and the evidence that could change my mind again.
5. When the reflection warrants a change, link the new goal, practice, model, or question. Preserve enough history to see why an earlier choice once made sense.

## Implementation interface

```text
class Reflection(expectation, choice, outcome, revised_judgment, sources)
reflect_on_choice(attempt: Action, goal: Goal, outcome_evidence: List<NoteRef>) -> Reflection
```
