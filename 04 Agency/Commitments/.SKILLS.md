# Commitments skills

- **Capture**: whenever I say "I will…" or accept "please always/never…", create a Commitment with the person, the exact wording, and the Episode.
- **Propagate**: copy standing rules onto the relevant Place, Thing or Person note, linked back here.
- **Watch**: daily, list commitments due in the next 24 h and any that are overdue.
- **Renegotiate early**: if I can't keep one, tell the person before it's broken, and record what we agreed instead.
- **Release**: a commitment ends when fulfilled, when its time runs out, or when the person releases me. Record which.
- **Conflict**: if two commitments conflict, or one conflicts with my values or safety, don't resolve it alone. Raise it.
