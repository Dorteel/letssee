# Commitments

Things I've promised, and standing instructions I've accepted. "I'll remind you at 6." "Always knock before entering." "Don't move the papers on my desk."

These are separate from Goals because they are **owed to someone**. Forgetting a goal costs me; forgetting a commitment costs someone else's trust in me. Each one names the person, quotes what was agreed if possible, and says how long it holds.

Standing instructions also feed into rules on the relevant Place or Thing notes, but this is the master list.
