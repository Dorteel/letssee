# Goals

Keep one desired outcome per note. A goal can be brief, like reaching the dock before my battery runs low, or enduring, like making my movements predictable to a person walking beside me. Record why I adopted it and whose request or which experience shaped it.

Give a goal a recognizable completion condition or, for an ongoing commitment, a recurring way to check how I am doing. Record competing commitments here so I can recover why I chose one over another.
