# Goals skills

- **Intake**: see parent `.SKILLS.md`. Always fill `source`, `why`, and `done_when`.
- **Clarify**: if `done_when` can't be stated, the goal is ambiguous. Ask the person rather than guess.
- **Prioritise**: safety > commitments > requests from people present > standing goals > self-maintenance > curiosity. Self-maintenance moves up when my body is impaired.
- **Review**: daily, check active goals for progress. Goals with no progress for a week are either re-planned, set to `waiting` with a reason, or dropped with a reason.
- **Close**: set `done` or `dropped`, link the Outcome.
