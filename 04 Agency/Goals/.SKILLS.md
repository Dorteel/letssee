# Form and revisit a goal

- Start from a request, an unmet need, or a reflection. Record its origin and express the desired outcome in terms I can later observe.
- Identify a tradeoff and what would make the goal worth pausing or revising. Link related goals where they exist.
- When I choose to act, create an action linked to the goal. Leave the goal open until there is evidence for its completion.
- Date each change of state and give its reason. For an abandoned goal, retain what changed. A later reader should be able to tell a change of mind from a successful attempt.

## Implementation interface

```text
class Goal(outcome, motive, completion_check, state, review_history, sources)
propose_goal(outcome: Text, completion_check: Text, origins: List<NoteRef>) -> Goal
review_goal(goal: Goal, state: Text, reason: Text, evidence: List<NoteRef>) -> Goal
```
