# Form and revisit a question

### Choose a response

`deliberate(question, motive, evidence, now) -> intention`

Read the source notes and check which situation, camera pose and time they describe. State what remains uncertain and why resolving it matters. Consider an answer from memory, another observation at no physical cost, and a camera look. If a nearer object hides the relevant surface, rotating a fixed camera may keep it hidden. A look can still centre the visible patch and improve its image detail if the camera supports that expectation. Record the reason for the chosen response and an alternative that might work if it fails.

For a look, use a calibrated mapping from image direction and current head pose to an absolute yaw and pitch. The body provides the calibrated mount convention. Missing pose, an untrusted clock or a stale source leaves the question open for later observation or operator input. Give the candidate a short expiry. Commit the intention with the source links, test and intended angles. A committed intention is available for deliberation and recall.

### Test the prediction

`reconsider(intention, attempt, later_percepts) -> successor_or_no_change`

Read the latest intention in its supersession chain. Compare the test with percepts from the same scene after the look, checking observation time, pose and whether the proposed distinguishing feature was actually visible. A body result that the camera reached its angle supplies evidence about the head. A fresh image showing the expected rim supplies evidence about the cup. If time or scene cannot be matched, preserve the open question.

When the answer or choice changes, append a successor with `supersedes` pointing to its predecessor and `sources` naming the evidence used. Mark it `satisfied` when its stated test passes, or `withdrawn` when the question ceases to matter. An inconclusive look can leave the original note open. Another look needs a new deliberation, a fresh intention version and a live request. When a chain has competing successors, read each current head before admitting further movement. A fresh deliberation can merge them by linking one head in `supersedes` and the other heads in the `also_supersedes` list and recording why the merged decision follows.
