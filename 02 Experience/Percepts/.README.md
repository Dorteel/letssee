# Percepts

A percept records one possible reading of a short stretch of sensory input. A rise in wrist current and a scraping sound might indicate contact with the drawer. The readings belong in `01 Sensing`. The proposed contact belongs here.

Give each percept a time window, a frame of reference when position matters, and links to the capture or individual streams used. Where a stream is long, identify the frame, sample range or offset that another reader could revisit. One percept may draw on several senses, and the same sample may support competing readings.

Use language that preserves the difference between a measured feature and an inferred object or event. Reserve the longer sequence of attempted movement, contact and release for an episode.
