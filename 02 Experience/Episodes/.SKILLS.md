# Episodes skills

- **Segment**: open and close episodes by the boundary rules in the parent `.SKILLS.md`.
- **Narrate**: write the episode as a short story in the first person: setting, intention, what happened, how it ended.
- **Evaluate**: compare outcome with intention and set `outcome` to `succeeded`, `partly`, `failed`, `abandoned` or `interrupted`. Failed and surprising episodes are always passed to `04 Agency/Outcomes`.
- **Extract**: list candidate facts (→ Understanding), candidate skill updates (→ Agency/Skills), and anything about myself (→ Self/Reflections).
- **Revisit**: when an episode is later used as evidence or recalled, bump its salience slightly. Memories that keep being useful should stay.
