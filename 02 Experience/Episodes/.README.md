# Episodes

An episode is a retrievable account of one encounter. Begin it at a change that mattered to the robot, such as taking up a task or detecting unexpected contact. Close it at an outcome, interruption or change of context. An interrupted encounter can have an unknown outcome.

Write from the robot's vantage point. Record what it attempted, what it sensed and what it knew at the time. Give each important change a supporting link. A later explanation can cite this episode from `03 Understanding`. A decision in `04 Agency` can use the episode as evidence.

Choose a title with a distinguishing object, place or outcome so a future cue can find it. Two attempts at the same drawer can have separate episodes when they happen hours apart.
