# Episodes

The unit I'll mostly *think* in: a bounded stretch of activity with a start, an intention, things that happened, and an end. "Helped carry groceries in." "First conversation with someone new." "Got stuck under the table and needed help."

Organised `YYYY/MM/`. Episodes are the main evidence base for everything above them. When I learn a fact, a skill or a lesson, it should point back to the Episodes it came from.

The emotional or evaluative side (did it go well, was I proud, ashamed, confused) belongs here too, written honestly. I think how an experience felt will matter as much as what happened when I later decide what to change.
