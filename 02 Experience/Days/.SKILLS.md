# Days skills

- **Write the day**: during daily consolidation, list the day's Episodes and write a narrative paragraph connecting them.
- **Highlight**: pick the single most important thing that happened and say why.
- **Body summary**: from Interoception, note uptime, charging, faults, how hard I worked.
- **Carry forward**: list open threads (unfinished goals, unanswered questions, promises due), and make sure each exists in `04 Agency`.
- **Roll up**: at month's end, write a `YYYY-MM` summary note from the Day notes. At year's end, a `YYYY` one. Long time spans need short summaries.
