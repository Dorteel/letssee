# Experience

A faint scrape can become a percept, then part of an episode about opening a drawer. Days later, a similar sound might bring that episode to mind. These three notes answer different questions.

| Location | Question | What belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Percepts/` | What might I be sensing? | A local interpretation tied to particular readings. |
| `Episodes/` | What happened? | A bounded sequence with context, actions and an outcome. |
| This folder | What did this cue bring to mind? | A recall note recording the cue, the search and what the retrieved material supports. |

Use a recall note when remembering influences a decision, exposes a gap or gives rise to a question worth keeping. Put the note's creation time in `created`. When writing later, record the time of recall under Cue. The remembered event keeps its own time in its episode. A recall can cite several episodes, or record a search that found none.
