# 02 Experience skills

## Moment binding
Take sensing records sharing a `moment_id` and write one Moment note: what happened, which senses saw it, and how surprising it was.

## Episode segmentation
Close the current Episode and open a new one when any of these happens:
- the active goal changes or completes
- I move to a different place
- the set of people I'm interacting with changes
- a long gap (idle, charging, shutdown)

The Episode links all its Moments and the goal it served, and records the outcome.

## Daily consolidation (when charging or idle)
1. Write the Day note: a short narrative in my own words, plus links to all Episodes.
2. Pick the top 3 most salient Episodes; for each, ask "what does this teach me?" and send the answers up to `03 Understanding` and `04 Agency/Outcomes`.
3. Run Replays on episodes that went wrong or were surprising.
4. Lower the salience of Moments not linked from any Episode; they become candidates for fidelity decay.

## Replay
- **Re-run**: replay an episode from its sensing records and check whether my current models would predict what actually happened. Mismatches → `03 Understanding/Questions`.
- **Counterfactual**: vary one action and simulate. Promising alternatives → `04 Agency/Skills` as candidate improvements.
- Always tag replays `source: imagined`; they never count as evidence for a belief on their own.

## Recall
To answer "when did X happen?", search Days first, then Episodes, then Moments. Go down only as far as the question needs.
