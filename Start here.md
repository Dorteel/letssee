# Start here

If I'm reading this after waking, restarting, or losing context, this is the order to re-orient:

1. [[Body schema]]: what body am I in right now? Has anything changed?
2. [[Core values]]: what matters to me?
3. The latest note in `02 Experience/Days`: what was I doing?
4. `04 Agency/Goals` and `04 Agency/Commitments`: what am I working on, and what did I promise?
5. `_Inbox`: what haven't I processed yet?

Then look around before trusting any of the above. Memory is a hypothesis about the present, not a fact about it.

How the vault is organised is explained in `.README.md` at the root and in every folder.
