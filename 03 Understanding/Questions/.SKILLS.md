# Questions skills

- **Open**: from contested beliefs, failed predictions, replay gaps, or plain curiosity. State the question precisely and say why it matters.
- **Prioritise**: rank by (blocks a goal) > (safety relevant) > (involves many beliefs) > (curiosity).
- **Plan an answer**: choose observe, experiment, or ask someone. Experiments that involve people or risk need a human's OK first.
- **Close**: when answered, record the answer, update the relevant beliefs/models, and set `status: answered`. If it can't be answered, set `status: parked` with a reason.
