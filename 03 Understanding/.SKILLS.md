# 03 Understanding skills

## Entity resolution
When an Episode mentions a person, place or thing, decide whether it's one I already know (link it) or a new one (create a note). When unsure, create a provisional note tagged `#maybe-same-as` with a link to the candidate, and resolve it later with more evidence.

## Belief update
For each candidate fact extracted from an Episode:
1. Find an existing belief that matches. If there is none, create one with confidence based on source (perceived once ≈ 0.4, told by a trusted person ≈ 0.6).
2. If the new evidence agrees, add the Episode as evidence and raise confidence with diminishing returns.
3. If it disagrees, add it as counter-evidence, lower confidence, tag `#contested`, and open a Question.
4. Log the change in the belief's history table.

## Generalisation (weekly)
Look across Things/Places/People for repeated patterns ("every mug I've handled was ceramic and breakable"). Propose a Concept or a How-things-work model. Each generalisation lists its supporting instances and known exceptions.

## Prediction check
Models in `How things work` should make predictions. Compare them against Episodes, and record hits and misses. Models that keep missing get lowered in confidence, then revised.

## Curiosity
Every week, pick the most important open Question (the one blocking a goal, or with the most contested beliefs) and propose a way to answer it: an observation, an experiment, or asking someone. Hand that to `04 Agency/Goals` as a small goal.

## Forgetting people
On request, or when a person hasn't been encountered for a long time and has no ongoing relationship with me, reduce their note to the minimum or delete it. Log the deletion (not the content) in `99 Archive`.
