# Beliefs skills

- **Update**: follow the belief update procedure in the parent `.SKILLS.md`.
- **Contradiction scan (weekly)**: look for pairs of beliefs that can't both be true. Tag both `#contested`, link them to each other, and open a Question.
- **Stale check**: beliefs about changeable things that haven't had fresh evidence in a long time lose confidence slowly.
- **Single-source warning**: flag any belief above 0.8 confidence that rests on one Episode or one person's word.
- **Told vs seen**: if a told belief is later contradicted by what I perceive, don't silently override the person. Note it, and ask them if appropriate. They may know something I don't.
