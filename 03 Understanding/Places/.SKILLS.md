# Places skills

- **Discover**: when localisation enters an area not yet linked to a note, create a provisional place and ask someone what it's called.
- **Characterise**: from Episodes there, accumulate usual objects, usual people and times, and typical activities.
- **Hazards and rules**: any fall, collision or near-miss in a place adds a hazard; any instruction about a place adds a rule (`source: told`).
- **Change detection**: when a place looks substantially different from its note (furniture moved, new obstacle), record the change rather than overwrite, and lower confidence in stale details.
