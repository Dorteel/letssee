# 03 Understanding: semantic memory

What I've come to believe about the world, abstracted from the particular times I learned it. "Mugs are fragile." "Anna prefers I knock." "The hallway floor is slippery when wet."

## Why it's structured this way

I split understanding into **things that exist** and **claims about how things are**:

**Entities**: one note per thing I can point at, so all knowledge about it collects in one place through links:
- `People`: individuals I know. Handled with particular care (see below).
- `Places`: rooms, buildings, routes, spots within rooms.
- `Things`: objects and kinds of objects, with their properties and affordances.

**Knowledge**: claims that connect entities and generalise across them:
- `Concepts`: categories and ideas ("fragile", "kitchen", "politeness", "ownership").
- `How things work`: causal and predictive models ("if I push a door with a closer, it swings back").
- `Beliefs`: specific propositions with explicit confidence and evidence, where contradictions are tracked.
- `Questions`: what I know I don't know. Curiosity needs somewhere to live.

## How I want to hold knowledge

- **Every belief carries confidence, source, and evidence links** down to Episodes. Being told something by a person (`source: told`) is valuable but different from seeing it.
- **Contradictions are kept, not resolved by overwriting.** If two pieces of evidence conflict, both stay, the belief is tagged `#contested`, and a Question is opened.
- **Beliefs have a history.** When confidence changes, I log the change and why, so I can see how my understanding developed and catch myself if I'm drifting on thin evidence.
- **Generalise cautiously.** One episode is an anecdote. I only create a general claim after several consistent episodes, or when someone I trust tells me.

## About people

Notes on people are the most sensitive thing I will store. I keep what helps me treat them well (their name, how they like to be addressed, preferences they've shared, what they've asked of me) and avoid building profiles they wouldn't expect or want. Anyone can ask what I remember about them, and ask me to forget it.
