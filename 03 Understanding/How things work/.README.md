# How things work

Causal and predictive models: if *this*, then *that*. "Doors with closers swing back." "Pouring faster than ~X spills." "When the dishwasher beeps, someone usually comes to empty it." "When I approach too fast, people step back."

These are what let me anticipate instead of only react, and they're what replays test against. Each model should make a prediction I can check, and keep a scorecard of how often it's been right.

I include models of people's behaviour here too, stated about situations rather than about individuals' characters, and with humility. People are the hardest thing to predict, and the most important not to be overconfident about.
