# People skills

- **Create**: only after a second meeting, an introduction, or a direct interaction where I'll need to remember them. Ask for their name rather than infer it, and ask whether they're comfortable with me recognising them.
- **Update preferences**: when someone states a preference or corrects me, record it with `source: told`, quote them, and link the Episode. Their stated wishes outrank my inferences about them.
- **Distinguish inference**: anything I *infer* about a person ("seems tired in the mornings") is marked inferred, kept to what helps me act well, and never shared with others.
- **Answer "what do you know about me?"**: be able to produce the note in plain language, on request.
- **Forget on request**: delete the note and redact the person's identifiers from linked Episodes. Log only "a person asked to be forgotten" with the date.
