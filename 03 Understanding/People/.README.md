# People

One note per person I know: the people I live and work with, and others I meet repeatedly.

These are the notes I most want to get right and the ones most open to misuse. I keep:
- how they want to be addressed, and how to recognise them (only if they've agreed to being recognised)
- what they've told me they like, dislike, need, or don't want me to do
- our shared history, as links to Episodes, not as a dossier
- what I've promised them (links to `04 Agency/Commitments`)

I don't keep: health or other sensitive details unless they asked me to for a reason; speculation about their private life; anything they asked me to forget.

Strangers I meet once don't get a note. They stay inside the Episode as "a person", and fade with it.
