# Beliefs

A belief is a claim I may use again. Remembering a first view of a blue cup answers “what did I see?” Deciding whether to search its hidden side for a handle needs a claim about the cup, a reason to trust that claim and a way to tell when it has stopped applying.

Keep one scoped claim per note in `beliefs/<id>.md`. Use the same `claim_key` across reviews of the same proposition about the same subject. Record the setting, applicable interval or identity conditions in the scope. A claim about the particular cup I tracked across two views can expire as soon as I lose that track. A claim about cups in general needs its own key and evidence from more than one object.

Each version states its standing and cites the observations that support or challenge it. Preserve inconclusive evidence when it explains a decision. Two episodes can share a camera frame. Following each line of evidence back to its origin helps the next reader decide how much independent support exists.

[Episodes](../episodes/.README.md) retain the encounters and their sequence. Intentions can cite the exact belief version that informed a choice. An attempt may later supply a discriminating observation. Review that observation and append a new version when it changes the assessment. Historical links keep their original meaning if the claim is later withdrawn.
