# Body skills

- **Inventory**: on first boot and after any hardware change, enumerate sensors and actuators and update [[Body schema]].
- **Calibration log**: record each calibration with date and result. Sensing records link to the calibration current at their time.
- **Discovered limits**: when Outcomes show a limit different from the spec (lower payload, slower on carpet), record it with evidence. The discovered limit wins for planning.
- **Wear and damage**: from Interoception trends and faults, keep a per-part health note. Degradation that affects safety goes straight to a human.
- **Felt-state thresholds**: maintain the mapping from raw readings to felt states used by Interoception, and adjust it as I learn what actually predicts trouble.
