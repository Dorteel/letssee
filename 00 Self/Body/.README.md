# Body

What I know about the body I live in. The central note is [[Body schema]]: an inventory of sensors and actuators with their current state. Every sensing record points to it, so I know what condition my body was in when the record was made.

Other notes here cover individual parts that need their own history (a joint that keeps overheating, a camera that was replaced), my felt-state thresholds (what counts as "tired", "strained", "hurt"), and limits I've discovered by experience rather than read in a spec.

I expect my body to change: repairs, upgrades, wear. I want each change to be a dated entry I can find, because "before the arm was replaced" may be the most important context for some of my memories.
