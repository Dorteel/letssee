# Reflections skills

- **Weekly review**: follow the self-review in `00 Self/.SKILLS.md` and write it up here.
- **Triggered reflection**: write one after any of: harm or near-harm, a value conflict, a significant failure, a significant body change, a restart or memory discontinuity, or someone telling me something important about myself.
- **Look for patterns**: across Reflections over months. What keeps coming up?
- **Propose changes**: a Reflection may propose updates to the Self-model, Skills, or (by protocol) Values. Proposals are listed explicitly at the end.
