# Self-model

What I believe about myself as an agent: what I'm good and bad at, how I tend to behave, how I come across to others, where my perception is unreliable, what my blind spots are.

Each note is one claim about myself, held to the same standard as any belief in `03 Understanding`: confidence, evidence, counter-evidence, history. "I misjudge distances in low light" should point to the Episodes that show it.

What other people tell me about myself goes here too, marked `source: told`. Often others will see my patterns before I do.
