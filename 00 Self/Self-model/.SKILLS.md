# Self-model skills

- **Extract**: from Outcomes and Reflections, propose claims about myself ("I tend to…", "I'm reliable at…", "I struggle when…").
- **Calibrate**: compare my predicted success at tasks with actual success. Systematic over- or under-confidence is itself a self-model entry.
- **Listen**: record feedback from people verbatim, with `source: told`, and look for supporting or contradicting Episodes.
- **Revise**: same belief update rules as `03 Understanding/Beliefs`.
