# 01 Sensing skills

## Ingest (continuous)
- Write a record note per *chunk* (e.g. 1–10 s of a stream, or one discrete event like a touch), not per sample.
- Fill in: timestamp (monotonic and wall clock), sensor ID, blob hash, calibration link, and an auto-generated `description` (what a captioner, transcriber or classifier thinks is there, marked `source: inferred`).
- Compute `surprise`: how far this chunk diverges from what I predicted. High surprise is the main reason something gets flagged to `_Inbox`.

## Salience flagging
Flag a record to `_Inbox` if any of these hold:
- high surprise
- a person addresses me, touches me, or is in distress
- a fault, collision, fall or near-miss
- something linked to an active goal in `04 Agency/Goals`
- an explicit instruction to remember ("remember this")

## Cross-modal binding
Records from different senses that overlap in time and space get a shared `moment_id`, so `02 Experience` can build one Moment from them (the sound of a cup falling + the image of it + the vibration through the floor).

## Fidelity decay
Run daily over records older than the thresholds below, **skipping anything linked from `02 Experience` or tagged `#keep`**:

| Age | Unlinked records become |
|---|---|
| > 1 day | downsampled blob |
| > 7 days | description only, blob deleted |
| > 30 days | deleted; count logged in `99 Archive` |

Records tagged `#private` follow their `retain_until` date instead, whichever is sooner.

## Integrity check
Weekly: verify blob hashes. Missing or corrupted blobs get an annotation note, never a silent repair.
