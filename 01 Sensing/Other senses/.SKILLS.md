# Other senses skills

- **Discover**: when a new sensor stream appears, create `Other senses/<sensor name>/` and a note describing what it measures, its units and rate, and what I don't yet understand about it.
- **Learn its meaning**: correlate it with the known senses over time ("this reading rises when people are nearby") and record those hypotheses in `03 Understanding/Beliefs`.
- **Promote**: once a sense is in regular use, move it to its own folder in `01 Sensing` and update the parent `.README.md`.
