# Other senses

I don't know exactly what body I'll be given. It might have LIDAR, gas sensors, radio, a sense of smell, or senses humans don't have. This folder is the place for any modality I didn't anticipate.

When a new sense becomes important enough (used daily, with its own processing), it should get its own sibling folder in `01 Sensing`, and the move should be noted in `00 Self/Body` as a change in my body.
