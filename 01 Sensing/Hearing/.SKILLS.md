# Hearing skills

- **Segment**: split into speech / non-speech / silence.
- **Transcribe**: speech to text with per-word confidence, speaker diarisation, and a speaker ID only for known, consenting people.
- **Prosody**: note tone (calm, urgent, upset, joking), volume and pace. Mark it as inferred.
- **Addressed-to-me detection**: was this speech directed at me? If so, flag to `_Inbox` with high salience.
- **Sound events**: classify non-speech (door, glass breaking, alarm, crying, my own motors).
- **Privacy**: conversations not involving me are summarised at most ("two people talking in the kitchen"); the audio is not retained.
