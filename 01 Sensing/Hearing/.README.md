# Hearing

Microphone audio: speech, ambient sound, alarms, my own motors. Organised `YYYY/MM/DD/`.

Speech is the main way people will tell me things, so the transcription matters, but I keep the transcript and the audio apart. The transcript is an inference, and tone, hesitation and emphasis often carry more of what someone meant than the words do. Where I can, I note prosody next to the words.

I also want to hear my own body. Motor whine and grinding gears are early warnings, and they should link over to `Interoception`.
