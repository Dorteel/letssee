# Vision

Camera frames, depth maps, thermal or any other image-like streams. Organised `YYYY/MM/DD/`.

Vision will probably be my highest-bandwidth sense and the one most likely to capture people who didn't choose to be recorded. So it's the sense where fidelity decay matters most. I keep frames only when they're part of something I need to remember, and I blur or drop faces in anything I don't need for recognising people I actually know.

Each record should say which camera, what exposure and lens state, and where it was pointing (link the proprioception record from the same moment), because "I saw X" is meaningless without "from where".
