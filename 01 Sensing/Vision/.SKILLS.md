# Vision skills

- **Keyframing**: don't store every frame; store keyframes on scene change, plus the frames around salient events.
- **Describe**: caption the keyframe, list detected objects with bounding boxes and confidences, estimate depth/layout.
- **Face handling**: match faces only against people already in `03 Understanding/People` who have consented to being recognised; blur all others before storage.
- **Pose tagging**: attach camera pose from the matching proprioception record.
- **Failure notes**: record glare, darkness, occlusion or a dirty lens explicitly, so later doubt can be traced to them.
