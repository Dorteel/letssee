# Touch

Tactile sensors, force/torque at the wrists and feet, bumpers, contact events. Organised `YYYY/MM/DD/`.

Touch is the sense where I meet the world physically, and where I can hurt someone or be hurt. I treat every unexpected contact as salient. I also want to remember how things *felt*: how heavy, slippery, soft or fragile they were, because that's what my grasping and handling skills in `04 Agency/Skills` will learn from.

Being touched by a person (a tap, a guiding hand, a push) is communication as much as sensation, and should be recorded as both.
