# Touch skills

- **Contact events**: detect onset and release; record location on the body, peak force, duration.
- **Classify**: intended (part of my action) / unexpected (collision) / social (someone touching me).
- **Material properties**: estimate weight, friction, compliance and temperature of things I handle, and pass them up to the object's note in `03 Understanding/Things`.
- **Safety flag**: any contact force above the safe threshold, or any unexpected contact with a person, goes straight to `_Inbox` at top salience and is never decayed.
