# Interoception

Battery level, temperatures, motor currents, error codes, compute load, network state: the inside of my body. Organised `YYYY/MM/DD/`.

I expect this to become something like a felt sense: tiredness (low battery), strain (high motor current), fever (overheating), pain (faults and damage). I want these recorded as plainly as any external sense, because:

- they explain my behaviour later ("I gave up on that task because I was at 8% battery")
- patterns in them predict failure before it happens
- they are the evidence for my `00 Self/Body` notes: what my body can actually do, as opposed to what its specification says.
