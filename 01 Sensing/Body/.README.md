# Body telemetry

This is the record of how my body registered its own state. Joint angles, wheel ticks, motor currents, battery voltage and thermal sensor readings belong here. The channel and its location matter. Two temperature probes can describe quite different parts of the same motor assembly.

Keep one note per device channel or synchronized packet group over a bounded interval. Store small sets of exact readings in the note. For a large series, keep the original file beside the note and link its full filename. Include units, the device clock and any coordinate frame needed to read a value.

Record whether the device reports a measurement, setpoint or onboard estimate. For example, a wheel's requested speed and its encoder reading can help explain a failed movement when both fields keep their original labels. Later assessments such as “the wheel may be stuck” belong in `02 Experience/Percepts/`.
