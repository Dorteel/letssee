# Captures

A capture preserves one sample as the sensor driver delivered it. Keep one note per retained sample, named `captures/<id>.md`, and place its serialized ROS message beside it as `<id>.cdr`. Keeping the complete message lets a future reader recover such details as image row padding, channel order and joint names. The note can be searched without opening the binary.

For a camera looking at a tray, this note answers “which frame could I inspect again?” A percept can point to the patch of blue in that frame and explain what object it might belong to. [Percepts](../percepts/.README.md)

Keep three distinct times when available. The acquisition stamp places the sample in the sensor's clock domain and epoch. UTC receipt time describes its arrival at the recorder. `recorded_at` describes when the note was prepared for storage. Two stamps on opposite sides of a simulation reset belong to different epochs. A clock domain with no known mapping to UTC remains useful for ordering samples within its own epoch.

A note can also mark an observed gap or the withholding of a payload. Record such metadata only when local recording policy permits it. A gap names the stream and the interval that went unobserved. Deliberate downsampling is described by the sampling period on the retained records. Anyone granted filesystem access to this vault could open its attachments, so the recorder checks the permitted audience before preserving bytes.
