# A memory I can question

I want to be able to say “I thought the cup was on the tray because I saw this edge,” then open the image and change my mind. I also want to know whether I looked again, whether the camera actually turned, and what the next image showed. Those are different things to remember. This vault gives each one an address.

The repository root is the Obsidian vault root. Each row below leads to the specification for a collection. Within each collection, `.README.md` explains what belongs there, `.SKILLS.md` describes the work, `.TEMPLATE.md` gives the note format, and `.BLUEPRINT.md` defines its ROS 2 integration. The dotfiles are design documents available in a file editor. Memories use ordinary Markdown filenames so a future reader can follow their links in Obsidian. Folder-qualified wikilinks start at the vault root. [Obsidian internal links](https://obsidian.md/help/links)

## One encounter

1. An image shows a blue curved edge partly hidden by the tray rim. The capture keeps the image and its acquisition context. A percept links to the capture and records the visible edge and two readings of it, a cup or part of the tray.
2. An episode puts that glimpse beside the robot's measured camera position. A belief that a blue cup is on this tray stays tentative. Re-reading the episode ten times still yields one view.
3. The planner can leave the question open. If identifying the cup matters now, it can record an intention to look again and name a feature that would help, such as a handle adjoining the blue edge. A live request can then authorize the camera to turn.
4. The attempt records whether the head reached its requested angle. A later image may show a handle, give the same ambiguous view, or never arrive. A stationary camera mount can reframe an image without revealing anything behind the rim. Each outcome leaves a different record.
5. When asked later “Why did I think it was a cup?”, I can follow the belief to the episode, percepts, and original views. If my certainty repeatedly rises when a second image adds no evidence, I can propose a change to the belief review rule and try it against past encounters.

## What persists

The seven folders group records by the question they answer. Time, place, object, and task cut across them and are available through links and properties. A capture can support several percepts, and an episode can be relevant to more than one belief. Copying everything into a folder for each object would lose that shared ancestry.

The root can also hold an **orientation note**. An operator opens an inquiry there by naming the question and why it matters. A handoff records where that inquiry paused, links the most useful evidence and open intention, and says what would justify another look. Opening a handoff after a restart is a reason to check the current evidence and intention. The [root template](.TEMPLATE.md) defines its format.

A direct source link preserves why a record was made. A new version can correct it using `supersedes`, while a conflicting interpretation can stand alongside it. A missing sample, an unknown outcome, and evidence against a claim each carry different information. The [record and recall rules](.SKILLS.md) explain how to keep those distinctions when writing or retrieving a note. The [system blueprint](.BLUEPRINT.md) specifies how the live processes share the vault and how work resumes after interruption.
